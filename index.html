About RS Enterprises

RS Enterprises provides Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) services focused on accurate inspection, safety and dependable reporting. We aim to support clients with professional inspection solutions for industrial and engineering applications.

Our NDT Services

Inspection services can be customized according to project requirements.

Radiographic Testing (RT)

X-ray and gamma radiography for weld and component inspection.

Ultrasonic Testing (UT)

Ultrasonic inspection for internal discontinuities and weld evaluation.

Magnetic Particle (MT)

Surface and near-surface defect detection in suitable ferromagnetic materials.

Dye Penetrant (PT)

Surface-breaking defect inspection for suitable non-porous materials.

Visual Testing (VT)

Visual examination of welds, components and fabrication quality.
